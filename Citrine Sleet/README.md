**NAME:**  
Citrine Sleet
  
**Alias**  
TraderTraitor, Hidden Cobra, Hastati Group, SectorA01, G0032, NewRomanic Cyber Army Team, Whois Hacking Team, Appleworm, UNC4034, Slow Pisces, Group 77, UNC4899, Labyrinth Chollima, UNC2970, ATK 3, UNC4736, Jade Sleet, ITG03, Gleaming Pisces, Gods Apostles, AppleJeus, UNC577, DEV-0139, Gods Disciples, TA404, Guardians of Peace
  
**Description**:   
The organization's earliest attack activities can be traced back to 2020, suspected to be from North Korea, primarily targeting financial and cryptocurrency objectives, and delivering the AppleJeus or customized FudModule rootkit. There is an overlap with Lazarus, and it is suspected to be associated with the North Korean Reconnaissance General Bureau's Unit 121.
[summary]
Citrine Sleet is a state-sponsored cyber threat group associated with North Korea's Reconnaissance Bureau (RGB), focused on financial gain through crypto asset theft. Active since 2018, it employs supply chain attacks via PyPI packages, zero-day exploits targeting browsers, and social engineering. Key tactics include fake crypto platforms, recruitment phishing, and deploying lightweight RATs like PondRAT. Targets include developers, crypto exchanges, and financial institutions globally.
