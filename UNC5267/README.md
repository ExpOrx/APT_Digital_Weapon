**NAME:**  
UNC5267
  
**Alias**  
Tenacious Pungsan, Wagemole, Nickel Tapestry, WaterPlum, Jasper Sleet, WageMole, PurpleBravo, Storm-0287, Operation Contagious Interview
  
**Description**:   
The earliest attack activities of this APT group can be traced back to 2018. Suspected to be from North Korea, they primarily gain remote employment opportunities in various organizations by posing as non-North Korean nationals, in order to generate income for the North Korean regime. They also use the privileged access obtained through their jobs to carry out malicious cyber intrusions.

[summary]
UNC5267 is a North Korea-linked cyber threat group that infiltrates international organizations via identity fraud in remote IT positions. Active since 2018, members use fabricated resumes, AI-generated profiles, and social engineering to access corporate networks for data theft and financial gain. Operating across the U.S., China, Russia, and other regions, the group targets technology, cryptocurrency, and remote work sectors. Attack methods include phishing, identity theft, and malware deployment. Recent activities involve sophisticated attacks on cryptocurrency firms using legitimate developer credentials to sign malicious code.
