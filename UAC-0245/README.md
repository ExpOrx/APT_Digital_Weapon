**NAME:**  
UAC-0245
  
 
**Description**:   
UAC-0245 is an attack group discovered in September 2025. Its activities are linked to the known group UAC-0184. The group primarily targets Ukraine, and its attack methods involve using specially crafted XLL and LNK files to deliver malicious payloads.
