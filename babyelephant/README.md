**NAME:**  
babyelephant
  
**Alias**  
G0121, BabyElephant, Hardcore Nationalist, Rattlesnake
  
**Description**:   
The earliest attack activities of this organization can be traced back to 2017. It is suspected to originate from India, primarily targeting government, military, defense, diplomacy, nuclear energy, finance, education, and telecommunications in South Asian countries such as Pakistan, Bangladesh, and Sri Lanka. 
[summary]
BabyElephant is a cyber threat group with South Asian regional ties, closely linked to Sidewinder (APT-Q-39). It primarily targets China and South Asian governments, military entities, academic institutions, and research organizations. TTPs include macro-laced documents delivering Nim-based backdoors, VBS/BAT script execution chains, and legitimate domain spoofing for C2. First exposed in late 2021, its tools show persistent evolution, with C++ backdoor variants observed in 2023 targeting Myanmar.
